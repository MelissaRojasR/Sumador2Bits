## How it works

This design realices the sum of two binary numbers.


## How to test

Use the dipswitch to indicate the value of the two numbers and the sum will be indicated in the output.  

## External hardware

A 4 dipswitch element is needed for the input conexions and three LEDs are needed for the outputs.
